PART payload winch

Single-servo winch that lowers a payload under a 5 g limit and releases itself.

ContextPurdue Aerial Robotics
WhenSpring 2026
ToolsSolidWorks
StatusComplete

Replaced a rack-and-pinion gate concept with a 270° servo winch and a self-freeing TPU belt that unhooks from the spool pins. Became the team design. The three-pin belt is statically indeterminate; the 295 mm belt prints curved to fit the bed.
