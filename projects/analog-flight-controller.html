Analog flight controller

Quadcopter PID loops computed in op-amps, not firmware.

ContextPersonal project
When2026
ToolsKiCad, Simulink, ATtiny
StatusIn progress

Angle-to-rate loops on roll and pitch, rate-only on yaw, fed by an MPU-6050 through an ATtiny1614 and MCP4822 DACs. Board history: v1 → rev A → rev B, plus an SMT variant.
