PART ailerons and flaps

Hinge-moment sizing and printed construction for a 95 m/s airframe.

ContextPurdue Aerial Robotics
WhenFall 2026
ToolsOnshape, Python
StatusIn progress

Servo sizing

Hinge moments from a linearized thin-airfoil flap model (Roskam Part VI) at FAR 23 load factors — n = 10/3 rolling, n = 2 flaps down. Ruddervator modelled as a flat plate at its total angle to the flow, including an 8 m/s gust.

SurfaceWorst caseServo torque
Aileron95 m/s, 25° trailing edge down13.5 kgf·cm
Ruddervator95 m/s, 25°, 8 m/s gust10.2 kgf·cm
Flap40 m/s, 40° down4.1 kgf·cm

Construction

Printed shells with internal triangular ribs, turning on a bronze sleeve bushing at the hinge line. Control horn bolts on with M2 screws into heat-set inserts.
