Ping pong launcher

Ball flight with drag, Magnus lift and spin decay, and a solver that picks the shot.

ContextPersonal project
When2026
ToolsMATLAB
StatusSimulation

Flight integrated with drag, Magnus lift and spin decay. A solver works backward from a target cup to a launch speed and spin; the launcher side sizes the flywheels for a three-wheel head.
